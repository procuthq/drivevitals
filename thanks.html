Thank you for buying DriveVitals Pro

Your license key is on the purchase page and in the email from Polar, our payment partner. It starts with DV-.

Turn on Pro

  1. Copy your license key.
  2. Click the DriveVitals icon in the menu bar, then the gear button to open Settings.
  3. Open the License tab, paste the key under "Already bought it?" and click Activate.

The PRO badge appears next to DriveVitals and every Pro feature unlocks straight away. Your key works on up to 3 Macs.

Lost your key?

Open the link in your purchase email and sign in with the email you used. You'll find your key there, and you can free up a Mac you no longer use.

Need help?

Email support@drivevitals.app and include the email you bought with.
