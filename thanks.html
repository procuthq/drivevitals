Thank you for buying DriveVitals Pro

Two steps and Pro is on: get your license key, then paste it into DriveVitals.

1. Get your license key

  1. Click Get my license key below and enter the email you used to pay.
  2. Type the 6-digit code Polar, our payment partner, emails you.
  3. Under DriveVitals Pro license, copy your key. It starts with DV-.

Get my license key

2. Turn on Pro

  1. Click the DriveVitals icon in the menu bar, then the gear button to open Settings.
  2. Open the License tab, paste the key under "Already bought it?" and click Activate.

The PRO badge appears next to DriveVitals and every Pro feature unlocks straight away. Your key works on up to 3 Macs. Lost it later? The same Get my license key page always has it.

Moving to a new Mac?

In DriveVitals on the old Mac, open Settings, then License, and click Remove license from this Mac. Then turn on Pro on the new Mac the same way.

Need help?

Email support@drivevitals.app and include the email you bought with.
