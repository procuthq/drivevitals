Help test DriveVitals

DriveVitals is a free Mac app that lives in your menu bar and checks the drives you plug in. Many Macs quietly run fast SSDs at a fraction of their speed because of an older cable or hub, and macOS never tells you. DriveVitals does.

  • Shows the real speed every drive and SD card is connected at
  • Warns you when a cable or hub slows a drive to USB 2.0 speed
  • Runs a speed test with a plain-English verdict
  • Shows the health of your Mac's internal SSD
  • Names the app blocking an eject, with Force Eject when you need it

Light on your Mac: a 5.4 MB app that uses 0% CPU and about 42 MB of memory while it waits in the menu bar.

I'm testing it with a few Mac users before the public launch on 8 December. It takes about 5 minutes. Thank you for helping! — Darkhan

1. Install

  1. Download DriveVitals with the button below.
  2. Open the DMG and drag DriveVitals into Applications.
  3. Plug in an external drive, SSD or SD card and click the DriveVitals icon in your menu bar.
