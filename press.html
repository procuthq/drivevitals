In one sentence

DriveVitals is a Mac menu bar app that shows the real speed of every drive, SD card and cable, plus the health of your internal SSD, and explains in plain English what's slowing a drive down.

In a paragraph

Two USB-C cables can look identical and differ 26 times in speed. macOS never warns you when a fast SSD connects at USB 2.0 speed, so an older cable or a hub can quietly turn a 1,000 MB/s drive into a 40 MB/s one. DriveVitals shows the speed each drive actually negotiated, warns about slow cables the moment a drive is plugged in, tells you whether a drive will work on Windows, runs speed tests with a verdict, reports internal SSD health (life used, data written, temperature), and names the app that's blocking an eject. It's free, with an optional one-time Pro upgrade.
