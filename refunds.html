Refund policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

If DriveVitals Pro isn't right for you, you can get a full refund within 14 days of buying, for any reason.

How to ask for a refund

Email support@drivevitals.app from the email you bought with, or include your order email. We'll refund you through Polar, our payment partner, usually within two business days. The money returns to your original payment method; your bank may take a few more days to show it.

After a refund, the license key stops working and DriveVitals returns to the free version. Everything free keeps working.

After 14 days

If something isn't working, email us anyway. We'll help fix it, and we consider refunds case by case.
