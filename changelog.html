What's new

Version 1.2

28 November 2026

  • How to fix: for NTFS (read-only on a Mac), FAT32 (4 GB file limit) and Apple Partition Map drives, reformat as exFAT in one click, or follow the Disk Utility steps. Only the affected volume is erased when a drive has several.
  • SD cards: format to the SD standard (FAT32 up to 32 GB, exFAT above, with MBR), or use SD Card Formatter.
  • Drives with several volumes: each volume listed with its own format and free space, and Open lets you pick which one.
  • Clearer status icons.

Version 1.1

27 November 2026

  • Format and partition scheme for every drive, with plain-English tips: NTFS is read-only on a Mac, FAT32 can't hold files over 4 GB, and which formats work on Windows.
  • Smarter slow-connection warnings: DriveVitals now tells a drive whose own adapter is USB 2.0 apart from a slow cable, hub or port.
  • Show all USB devices: a clear type and icon for every device, each hub listed once, sorted by type.
  • The status icon now also warns when a drive is low on space.
  • Hover over GPT, MBR or Apple Partition Map to see what it means.

Version 1.0

27 November 2026 · first release

  • Every drive and SD card in the menu bar, with its real connection speed.
  • Slow-cable warnings when a drive connects at USB 2.0 speed.
  • Internal SSD health: life used, data written, temperature and hours.
  • Speed test with a plain-English verdict.
  • Eject, Mount and Force Eject, with the name of the app blocking an eject.
  • Automatic updates.
