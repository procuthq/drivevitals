Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

DriveVitals is made by DailyTweak. We built it to keep your information on your Mac.

The app

DriveVitals reads information about your drives, such as their names, connection speeds, free space and SSD health, to show it to you. This information stays on your Mac. Speed tests write a temporary file to the drive you test and delete it when the test ends.

The app has no analytics, no advertising and no account. It connects to the internet for two things only:

  • Checking for app updates.
  • If you buy Pro, verifying your license key with Polar. This sends your key and your Mac's name, so you can see which Macs use your license.

Purchases

Pro is sold by Polar Software, Inc., which acts as merchant of record and handles payment, tax and receipts. Polar's privacy policy applies to the information you give at checkout. We receive your email address and order details so we can support you.

This website

This site uses GoatCounter to count visits without cookies and without collecting personal data. It doesn't track you across other sites.
